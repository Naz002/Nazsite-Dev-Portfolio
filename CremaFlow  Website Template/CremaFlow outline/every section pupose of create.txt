1. Navbar

Navigation should be simple and easy to use.

2. Hero

This is the first impression.

Goal:

"I want to visit this café."

3. Trusted By / Statistics

Examples:

☕ 5,000+ Cups Served
😊 2,000+ Happy Customers
⭐ 4.9 Rating

This builds trust quickly.

4. Why Choose CremaFlow

Tell visitors why they should choose your café.

Examples:

Premium Coffee
Cozy Workspace
Fresh Pastries
Free Wi-Fi
5. Featured Coffee

Show your best-selling drinks.

Don't overwhelm visitors with the full menu here.

6. About Preview

A short story about the café.

Add a "Read More" button that links to the About page.

7. Today's Specials

This makes the homepage feel fresh and encourages visitors to explore.

8. Gallery Preview

Show 4–6 beautiful images.

Add a "View Gallery" button.

9. Testimonials

Social proof.

Real or demo testimonials make the business feel more trustworthy.

10. Reservation CTA

End with a strong action.

Example:

"Reserve your table today."
